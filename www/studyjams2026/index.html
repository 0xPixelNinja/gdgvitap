Study Jams 2026

Set up a public Google Skills profile, then register with the organiser code. Missing a step means missing the swags.

GDGC-VITAP Open the form
Use a new Google account if you already earned badges in an earlier year. The account must be created on or after 14 September 2026. Type the organiser code exactly as GDGC-VITAP.
Step 1

Sign in to Google Skills

Open skills.google and choose Sign in at the top right.

If you already have badges from a previous year, create a new account with a fresh email first.

Step 2

Authorize the account

Pick the Google account you want linked to Google Skills, then choose Continue.

Step 3

Accept the terms

Read the Terms of Service and choose I Accept so the dashboard opens.

Step 4

Open Settings

On the dashboard, open your profile picture at the top right, then choose Settings.

Step 5

Make the profile public

Under Public visibility, check Make profile public. This step is required. A private profile cannot be verified.

Step 6

Copy your profile link

After the box is checked, a green banner shows the public URL. Copy that exact link. You will paste it into the form later.

Step 7

Open the form

Read the instructions on the first page and enter the email on your Google Skills account.

Open the registration form

Step 8

Accept the form terms

Review the communication and tracking terms, then select Yes, I accept the terms.

Step 9

Enter your details

Enter your full name and gender. For learner category, select Student.

Step 10

Enter the organiser code

Under Choose your Organiser, select GDG on Campus Organizer. In the referral code box, type exactly GDGC-VITAP.

A blank or mistyped code means you will not receive the swags.

Step 11

Verify the account and email

Select I verify to confirm the account is new, created on or after 14 September 2026.

Check the email twice. It must match the Google Skills account exactly.

Step 12

Paste your own profile URL

Do not paste the example URL shown on the form. Paste the unique public profile link you copied from the green banner.

Step 13

Acknowledge and submit

Choose I Acknowledge that the information is correct, and Yes, I Agree to complete the learning pathway. Then submit.

Done

You are enrolled

The confirmation screen means the form landed. Watch your inbox for the welcome email and access code from Google Cloud.
