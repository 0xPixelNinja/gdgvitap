Study Jams '27
Registration Guide

How to correctly configure your Google Skills profile, make it public, and secure your spot.

Pay strict attention: Missing a step means missing swags!
Step 1

Sign In.

Go to the Google Skills platform (skills.google).

Click on the Sign In button in the top right corner.

Pro Tip: Create a NEW account with a fresh email if you already earned badges in previous years!

Step 2

Authorize
Account.

Choose the Google account you want to link to Google Skills.

Click on Continue to grant access and proceed.

Step 3

Accept Terms.

Once authorized, ensure you review and click I Accept on the Terms of Service page to access your dashboard.

Step 4

Go to Settings.

Now that you are on your Dashboard, look at the top right corner.

Click on your Profile Avatar to open the dropdown menu, and click on Settings.

Step 5 â€¢ CRITICAL

Make it Public.

This is the most important step! Under the "Public visibility" section, you MUST check the box that says Make profile public.

Step 6

Copy Your Link.

Once you check the box, look for the green banner that says "Your profile is public and accessible at...".

Copy that exact URL link shown inside the green banner.

Scan & Register

Access the Form.

Now that your profile is public and you have your URL copied, it's time to register.

Scan the QR code on the right with your phone, or click the button below to open the form.

Open Registration Form →

Scan to Register

Step 7

Open the Form.

Scan the QR code provided by the GDG Team to open the Google Registration Form.

Read the instructions carefully and enter your registered email address.

Step 8

Accept the Terms.

Review the communication and tracking terms.

Select "Yes, I accept the terms" to proceed to the next page.

Step 9

Personal Details.

Enter your Full Name and Gender.

For the Learner Category, make sure you select Student.

Acknowledgements.

Almost done! Review the final declarations.
